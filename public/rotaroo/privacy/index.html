Rotaroo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Rotaroo is a paid app you buy once. The app is the product; your data is not. Rotaroo keeps the people you add, your call history and your settings on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. Jumblestep LLC cannot read that data, does not collect it, and runs no servers for Rotaroo. The only things we ever receive are anonymous, aggregate statistics and crash reports that Apple provides to every developer, which contain no names, phone numbers or contacts.

This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it lives, what we receive, and how to delete everything.

1. Who we are

Rotaroo is made by Jumblestep LLC, a New York limited liability company. For anything about this policy or your data, email rotaroosupport at jumblestep dot com. Jumblestep LLC is the data controller for the little data this policy covers.

2. What the app stores, and where

Rotaroo stores only what you put into it:

  • People you add: their name, the phone numbers and email addresses you chose to keep, and a link to the matching entry in your phone's Contacts so the app can notice if that entry is later removed.
  • Your call history: the dates of calls you placed from the app or logged by hand. The app never records calls or their contents; it only hands off to the Phone, FaceTime or WhatsApp app.
  • How you organize people: categories, snoozes, scheduled calls, and which number you prefer for each person.
  • Your settings: the daily reminder time, your default calling method, sort order, and whether you have completed setup.

All of it lives in two places, both yours:

  1. On your device, in the app's own storage.
  2. In your private iCloud, through Apple's CloudKit, so the same data returns if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone signed into the same Apple Account. This data is stored under your Apple Account in Apple's iCloud. Jumblestep LLC has no access to it and cannot read, export or recover it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Rotaroo uses no Jumblestep servers, databases or accounts. Nothing you add to Rotaroo is sent to us.

3. What we receive

We receive two kinds of information, both from Apple and both anonymous:

  • Aggregate statistics. Apple's App Store Connect shows us totals such as how many people downloaded Rotaroo, how many opened it on a given day, and how many sessions crashed. These are counts, not records of individuals. Usage figures include only people who have agreed to share analytics with developers in their iOS settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), and we cannot see or identify any one user.
  • Crash reports. If Rotaroo crashes and your iOS settings allow sharing with app developers, Apple sends us a technical report: the app version, the iOS version, the device model, and the point in the code where the crash happened. It does not include your contacts, call history, or anything you typed.

We use both only to find and fix problems in the app. You can turn either off at any time in the iOS Analytics & Improvements settings, and the app works the same either way.

4. What Rotaroo never does

  • Rotaroo has no user accounts and never asks you for a name, email address or password.
  • Rotaroo does not collect, store or send us your contacts, phone numbers, call history or anything else you put into it.
  • No Rotaroo data is sold, rented, shared or traded, because we hold none.
  • Rotaroo shows no advertising, uses no advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Rotaroo contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. The only code in it is ours and Apple's.
  • Rotaroo does not upload your Contacts list. Only the people you choose, one by one, are saved, and they are saved to your device and your own iCloud, not to us.

5. Permissions the app asks for

Permission Why Rotaroo asks If you decline
Contacts To show your Contacts so you can pick the people you want in Rotaroo, and to keep each added person linked to their Contacts entry. You can grant access to all contacts or only the ones you select; nothing is added automatically. You can still add people by typing their name and number.
Notifications To send the optional daily reminder and a reminder for calls you schedule. No reminders are sent; everything else works.

Rotaroo never asks for your location, microphone, camera, photos, or calendar.

6. Purchases

Rotaroo is sold through the App Store for a one-time price. Apple handles the purchase, your payment details and any refund; we never see your card, billing address or Apple Account details. Apple tells us only the aggregate number of sales. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

Because your data is on your device and in your iCloud, you delete it yourself, and it is gone for good:

  • Delete everything: in Rotaroo, open Settings → Delete Data and type DELETE. This removes every person, call and setting from the device and from your private iCloud. If iCloud cannot be reached at that moment, the app keeps retrying the iCloud part and tells you when it is done.
  • Delete one person: open their profile and tap Remove from Rotaroo. The person and their call history are removed from the device and from iCloud.
  • Delete the app: removes everything stored on the device. Your iCloud copy stays until you delete it from inside the app, or by removing Rotaroo's data under iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Stop iCloud sync: turn Rotaroo off under iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud. The app then keeps data on the device only.
  • Revoke a permission: Contacts and Notifications can be switched off at any time in iOS Settings → Apps → Rotaroo.

We keep no copy of your Rotaroo data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you email us a request anyway, we will confirm that within 30 days.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children; nothing a user puts into the app reaches us. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us at rotaroosupport at jumblestep dot com and we will delete it.

9. Your rights in the EU, UK, California and elsewhere

Rotaroo is sold worldwide. Wherever you live, you have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. In practice the answer to "what do you hold about me from Rotaroo" is: nothing that identifies you. The crash reports and statistics described in section 3 are anonymous, and we cannot link them to you even if you ask.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. The legal basis for receiving the anonymous statistics and crash reports in section 3 is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); you can stop them at any time in your iOS analytics settings. The app itself processes your contacts and call history only on your device and in your own iCloud, where Jumblestep LLC is not a recipient. We do not transfer personal data out of the EEA or UK because none reaches us. To exercise any right, email rotaroosupport at jumblestep dot com; you may also complain to your national supervisory authority (for the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

California. In connection with Rotaroo, we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act, and have not done so in the past 12 months. Rotaroo does not use sensitive personal information. Requests under California law can be sent to the same address, and we will not treat you differently for making one.

Other regions. Residents of Canada, Australia, Brazil and other countries with privacy laws have the same rights described at the top of this section; the same email address is the way to use them.

10. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your iPhone (its passcode and encryption) and of your Apple Account, including Apple's encryption of iCloud data in transit and at rest. We recommend two-factor authentication on your Apple Account. Because no data reaches Jumblestep LLC, a breach of our systems cannot expose your contacts or call history.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting something new, we will update this page, change the effective date at the top, and say so in the App Store release notes before the change takes effect. Continuing to use the app after that date means you accept the updated policy. Earlier versions are available on request.
