Staying connected

We design simple mobile applications that help people make time for the relationships that matter. Our first app, Rotaroo, is a playful way to make a habit out of calling friends and family. Stop doomscrolling and start talking to the people that you care about.

Collaboration tools for writers

We build focused software that helps writers work on stories and scripts with their teams, while protecting their intellectual property. More on this soon!

About Jumblestep

Jumblestep LLC is a software company. We aim to build applications that help people realize two of the original promises of technology: stay in touch with the people that matter, and build and collaborate on creative projects. We are Marc Hecht and Sagar Setru and based in New York City.
